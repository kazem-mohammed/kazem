Professional story

From operations to optimisation

A path built by doing the work — not by claiming it.

01

Beginning — inside the operation

I started in customer service at D2D Express in Port Said, registering incoming orders, requesting products from suppliers on Alibaba, Taobao and 1688, and tracking every shipment until it reached the customer. That job taught me how a business actually moves — and where it usually slows down.

02

Education — systems and IT

Studying at Port Said University, Faculty of Management Technology and Information Systems (IT Department), where the coursework is rooted in information systems. The degree runs 2023 to 2027.

03

Career development — into paid media

I trained at Creative Hub Port Said in Digital Marketing, AI & Digital Marketing and Sales, then moved into buying and running campaigns: targeted audiences, A/B tests, budget allocation and performance tracking.

04

Major projects — shipped for real businesses

Alongside the marketing work, I design and publish complete websites for restaurants, cafés and dessert brands. Six of them are live right now — each one a working digital menu, not a mock-up.

05

Current direction

Today I work as a Junior Media Buyer, supporting campaign performance and data accuracy, and at FlueroGen as a Data Scientist producing SEO content, multi-channel campaigns and KPI reporting. My aim is to keep growing on both sides of the work — media and data — and to build the digital experiences that support it.
